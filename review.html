Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Lenders pay me a commission when your loan settles, so you don’t pay me. I’ll show you what I’m paid before you sign anything.

Is switching always worth it?

No. Fees, and break costs on fixed loans, can outweigh a lower rate. If staying or asking your lender first is better, I’ll say so.

I’ve been told I can’t refinance. Can you help?

Maybe. Lenders test loans at a rate at least 3% above the actual one, and some assess a like-for-like refinance differently. Not everyone will qualify.

Will a review affect my credit score?

No. A review doesn’t involve a credit check. That only happens when you apply, and I’ll tell you before it does.

How long does it take?

Usually I can come back to you the same or next business day with the comparison.
