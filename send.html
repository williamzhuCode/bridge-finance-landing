Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Lenders pay me a commission when your loan settles, so you don’t pay me for the loan or the research. I’ll show you what I’m paid before you sign anything.

Do I have to do my loan with you?

No. There’s no obligation. If you do your home loan with me, the property research comes with it.

Is this a valuation?

No. It’s my opinion from recent sales, not a formal valuation. A lender’s valuation can come in differently.

Are you a buyer’s agent?

No. I don’t search, negotiate or bid for you. A full buyer’s agent in Sydney typically charges $8,000 to $20,000 and does a lot more than this.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually the same day. If you’re bidding this week, say so in the form or call me.

What happens to my details?

They go to me only, and only for this. No mailing lists.
