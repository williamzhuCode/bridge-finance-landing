Four ways around LMI - and who each suits

Government

5% Deposit Scheme

Buy with a 5% deposit and no LMI. No income cap, no limit on places.

Suits: first home buyers with 5% saved, buying under the area’s price cap.
Eligibility and property price caps apply. Stamp duty is separate. Checked 10 Nov 2026.
Family

Family guarantee

Parents use the equity in their home as extra security, so you can buy with a small deposit and no LMI.

Suits: buyers with strong income but a small deposit, and parents with equity who understand the risk.
The guarantor’s property is at risk if repayments aren’t made. Guarantors should get independent legal advice.
Your job

Professional waivers

Some lenders waive LMI for doctors, dentists and specialists up to 95%, and for nurses and allied health usually up to 90% with income minimums.

Suits: eligible registered professionals with at least 5-10% saved.
Depends on the lender, registration, income and deposit. Lender policies checked June 2026.
Lender

No-LMI lenders up to 90%

Some lenders don’t charge LMI up to 90% of the price, with no profession test.

Suits: buyers with 10% saved who’d rather pay a little more in rate than LMI upfront.
The rate is usually higher above 80%, so I compare the total cost, not just the LMI.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Lenders pay me a commission when your loan settles, so you don’t pay me. I’ll show you what I’m paid before you sign anything.

Is no LMI always the cheapest option?

No. Some no-LMI loans carry a higher rate, so I compare the total cost over the years you’re likely to keep the loan.

Will this affect my credit score?

No. Checking your options doesn’t involve a credit check. That only happens when you apply, and I’ll tell you before it does.

Do I need 20% saved?

Not always. The 5% scheme, a family guarantee or a professional waiver can mean buying with less and no LMI, if you’re eligible.

What’s the risk with a family guarantee?

The guarantor’s home is security for part of your loan, so it’s at risk if repayments aren’t made. Guarantors should get independent legal advice first.
